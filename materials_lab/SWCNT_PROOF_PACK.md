## 1. EXACT COMPOSITION & PROCESS STEPS

### Chemical Composition
- **Formula**: C (pure carbon, sp² hybridization)
- **Structure**: Single-walled cylindrical graphene sheets
- **Diameter Range**: 0.7-2.0 nm (1.2-1.5 nm typical)
- **Length**: 100 nm - 10 μm (aspect ratio 100-1000:1)
- **Chirality Distribution**: (n,m) indices determine metallic/semiconducting properties
- **Purity**: >90% SWCNT, <5% DWCNT, <5% amorphous carbon

### Scalable Synthesis Process: Chemical Vapor Deposition (CVD)

#### Step 1: Catalyst Preparation (2 hours)
```
Input Materials:
- Aluminum oxide (Al₂O₃, 10 nm thin film)
- Iron nitrate (Fe(NO₃)₃·9H₂O, 0.1M solution)
- Molybdenum nitrate (Mo(NO₃)₃·5H₂O, 0.01M solution)
- Silicon substrate (100 mm diameter)

Process Parameters:
- Deposition Method: Spin coating + calcination
- Temperature: 800°C (calcination)
- Atmosphere: Air, 2 hours
- Catalyst Loading: 0.1-1.0 mg/cm²
- Particle Size: 1-5 nm Fe/Mo nanoparticles
```

#### Step 2: CNT Growth (30 minutes)
```
Input Materials:
- Ethylene (C₂H₄, 99.9% purity)
- Hydrogen gas (99.999% purity)
- Argon gas (99.999% purity)

Process Parameters:
- Temperature: 850-950°C
- Pressure: Atmospheric (1 atm)
- Gas Flow: C₂H₄ (100 sccm), H₂ (400 sccm), Ar (100 sccm)
- Growth Time: 10-30 minutes
- Carbon Efficiency: 70-90%
- Growth Rate: 1-10 μm/minute
```

#### Step 3: Purification Process (4 hours)
```
Input Materials:
- Nitric acid (HNO₃, 3M)
- Hydrochloric acid (HCl, 3M)
- Hydrogen peroxide (H₂O₂, 30%)
- Sodium hydroxide (NaOH, 1M)

Process Steps:
1. Air oxidation: 400°C, 30 minutes (removes amorphous carbon)
2. Acid reflux: 3M HNO₃, 80°C, 2 hours (functionalizes and purifies)
3. Centrifugation: 10,000g, 30 minutes (separates by diameter)
4. Neutralization: pH adjustment and washing

Yield: 50-80% purified SWCNT
Purity: >90% SWCNT content
```

**Production Scale**: Lab (grams) → Pilot (kilograms) → Industrial (tons)  
**Equipment Cost**: $200K CVD system + $100K purification setup  
**Energy Consumption**: 50 kWh/kg (vs 200 kWh/kg for multi-walled CNTs)

## 3. MEASUREMENT & VALIDATION PROTOCOL (TEST PLAN)

### Primary Characterization Suite

#### A. Raman Spectroscopy (Structure & Quality)
```
Instrument: Horiba LabRAM HR Evolution
Laser: 633 nm, 1.5 mW power
Integration Time: 30 seconds
Key Features:
- Radial Breathing Mode (RBM): 100-300 cm⁻¹ (diameter fingerprint)
- G-band: 1590 cm⁻¹ (graphitic structure)
- D-band: 1350 cm⁻¹ (defects, target D/G <0.1)
- G'-band: 2600 cm⁻¹ (second order, diameter sensitive)
Standards: ASTM E2529, ISO/TR 19733
```

#### B. Transmission Electron Microscopy (Morphology)
```
Instrument: JEOL JEM-2100F (200kV)
Sample Prep: Dispersion in ethanol, drop-cast on lacey carbon grid
Imaging Modes:
- Bright field: Diameter and length distribution
- HRTEM: Wall structure, chirality analysis
- EELS: Chemical composition verification
Analysis:
- Diameter: 0.7-2.0 nm (log-normal distribution)
- Length: 100 nm - 10 μm
- Bundling: Individual tubes vs ropes
```

#### C. Atomic Force Microscopy (Individual CNT Analysis)
```
Instrument: Bruker Multimode 8
Mode: Tapping mode in air
Tip: Silicon probe with 2 nm radius
Scan Parameters:
- Scan size: 5×5 μm
- Resolution: 1024×1024 pixels
- Measurements:
  - Height: 0.7-2.0 nm (diameter)
  - Length: Individual CNT tracing
  - Mechanical properties: Force curves
```

### Performance Testing Protocols

#### Mechanical Testing
```
Method: Individual CNT tensile testing
Sample Geometry: CNTs bridged across trenches (2-5 μm gaps)
Loading: AFM tip or MEMS actuators
Data Acquisition: Force-displacement at 100 Hz
Analysis:
- Stress-strain relationships
- Weibull statistics for strength distribution
- Fracture mechanisms
Standards: ASTM E2546 (nanoscale mechanical testing)
```

#### Electrical Testing
```
Method: CNT network field effect transistors
Device Fabrication:
- CNT dispersion and alignment
- Electrode deposition (Ti/Au, 5/50 nm)
- Gate dielectric (SiO₂, 100 nm)
Measurements:
- I-V characteristics (-10 to +10 V)
- Transfer characteristics (V_g = -50 to +50 V)
- Mobility extraction using FET model
- Contact resistance analysis
```

#### Thermal Testing
```
Method: T-type suspended CNT thermal conductance
Sample Prep: Individual CNTs suspended across micro-trenches
Measurement:
- Electrical resistance heating
- Raman temperature calibration
- Heat dissipation modeling
Calibration: Reference materials (multi-walled CNTs, graphene)
Standards: ASTM E1461 adapted for nanoscale
```

- **Defect Density**: D/G ratio <0.1 (Raman)

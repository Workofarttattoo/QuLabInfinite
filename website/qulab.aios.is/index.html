The Problem

Physical R&D is slow, expensive,
and locked out of the AI era

Research teams spend months and hundreds of thousands of dollars on experiments that could be simulated in seconds. Meanwhile, AI agents have zero access to validated scientific tools.

Tiny databases, enterprise prices

COMSOL has 17K materials and charges $10K+/year. ANSYS Granta has 10K materials at $15K+/year. Researchers hit walls constantly.

Reference data, not simulation data

MatWeb has 120K materials but they're reference-only. You can't feed them into a simulation. Researchers waste days reformatting.

No AI integration path

GPT, Claude, and autonomous research agents are exploding. None of them can run a validated simulation. The tools don't have APIs.

Fragmented across domains

Physics in one tool, chemistry in another, biology in a third. No unified platform means no cross-domain discovery.

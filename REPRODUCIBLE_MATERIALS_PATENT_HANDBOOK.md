# 📂 CATEGORY 1: STRUCTURAL COMPOSITES & METAMATERIALS

## 🤖 MATERIAL 01: TITAN-WEAVE
**Classification:** Ultralight Titanium-Carbon Fiber Metal Matrix Composite (MMC)
**Patent Focus:** Novel interfacial bonding layer for zero-void MMC synthesis.

### 📝 Patent Logic
*   **Novelty:** Integration of a 55nm silicon-carbide (SiC) nanocoating on PAN-based carbon fiber Warp-to-Titanium-Weft weaving, preventing the formation of brittle TiC phases.
*   **Non-Obviousness:** Use of Flash Joule Heating (FJH) to "instant-sinter" the titanium matrix around the carbon weave in <500ms, effectively freezing the microstructure before oxidation occurs.
*   **Applications:** Aerospace fuselage, hypersonic leading edges, high-performance orthopedic implants.

### 🛠️ Synthesis Breakdown (How to Make It)
1.  **Fiber Preparation:** Take IM7 Carbon Fiber. Apply SiC nanocoating via Plasma-Enhanced CVD (PECVD) at 450°C for 20 minutes.
2.  **Weaving:** Create a 3D woven preform of SiC-coated carbon fiber (Warp) and Grade 5 Titanium wire (Weft).
3.  **Encapsulation:** Place the preform in a vacuum-sealed nitrogen-filled quartz reactor.
4.  **Flash Pulse:** Apply a high-voltage pulse (68V, 1200A) for 420ms. This melts the titanium just long enough to infiltrate the fiber weave without degrading the carbon.
5.  **Cooling:** Rapid N₂ quench at 200°C/sec.

## 🤖 MATERIAL 03: AERO-SPINE (Batch 02)
**Classification:** Nacre-Inspired Structural Ceramic Composite

### 📝 Patent Logic
*   **Novelty:** Mirroring the hexagonal platelet-and-polymer structure of natural nacre (seashells) using Al₂O₃ platelets and a graphene-modified epoxy.
*   **Applications:** Lightweight ballistic armor, high-strength aerospace skins.

### 🛠️ Synthesis Breakdown (How to Make It)
1.  **Platelet Alignment:** Use Dr. Blade casting or magnetic field alignment of Al₂O₃ alumina platelets (500nm thick) in a ceramic slurry.
2.  **Sintering:** Sinter at 1450°C to create a "brick-and-mortar" staggered ceramic skeleton.
3.  **Infiltration:** Vacuum-infiltrate the skeleton with a 0.5 wt% graphene-doped epoxy resin.
4.  **Curing:** Heat at 120°C for 2 hours under 5 MPa pressure.

**Classification:** Bioactive Piezoelectric Hydroxyapatite Scaffold

## 🤖 MATERIAL 06: NOVA-CELL (Batch 02)
**Classification:** Cesium-FA Perovskite Photovoltaic (PV)
**Patent Focus:** Passivation layer using Phenethylammonium Iodide (PEAI) for 26%+ efficiency.

### 🛠️ Synthesis Breakdown (How to Make It)
1.  **Solution:** Dissolve FAI, PbI₂, and CsI in a DMF/DMSO (4:1) solvent mixture.
2.  **Spin-Coating:** Spin-coat onto FTO/TiO₂ substrate at 4000 RPM.
3.  **Antisolvent:** Drip Chlorobenzene during spin-coating to trigger crystallization.
4.  **Passivation:** Apply a 5mM solution of PEAI in Isopropanol over the perovskite film.
5.  **Annealing:** 100°C for 10 minutes.
